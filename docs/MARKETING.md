# The First Cycle Edition marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Readers arriving from the film, a creator or a community post. **Offer.** Let a reader open Book I, hear the Grow Room recording and find the premiere from a single edition.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Film a phone opening the edition, reading a passage and pressing play on the song. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Open the free edition. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 20 qualified edition opens. |

### Sales test

Use the real tote listing in the book view as the first sales path. Target one fulfilled, attributable tote after fees and print cost. Measure the path to the existing bookstore or merchandise offer using confirmed orders where available.

### Measurement and decision

Use `first-cycle-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** The reviewed public package centers on reading and listening. NFT claims remain paused; wallet and sponsor operations stay in the recovery archive. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
